## What Clever Cleaner does well

Clever Cleaner is a good app, and it's honest about what it does:

- **It shrinks instead of only deleting.** Live Photos become stills, and videos, photos, and screenshots can be compressed.
- **No ads, no pop-ups**, which is rare among cleaners.
- **Everything runs on the phone.** Its terms say photos are analyzed with on-device models and never uploaded.
- **A one-time purchase.** $39.99 once is a fair deal if you'd rather not subscribe to anything.
- **A Skip List**, so photos you've decided to keep stop showing up.

If those are the things you care about most, it's a solid choice.
