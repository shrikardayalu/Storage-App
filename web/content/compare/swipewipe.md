## What Swipewipe does well

- **It makes a chore feel good.** Going month by month through old photos is half cleanup, half nostalgia, and the streaks and **On This Day** widget keep people coming back.
- **Undo is easy.** Swiping left only marks a photo, and you confirm everything on a review screen before anything is deleted.
- **Lots of ways in**: recents, random, your albums, or grouped duplicates.
- **A creative toolkit** with a subscription: AI photo enhancement, background removal, and compressing, trimming, or splitting videos.
- **Android too**, if your family is split across platforms.

## Swiping everything vs. swiping what matters

The catch with swiping every photo is the math. A library of 20,000 photos is 20,000 decisions, and most of them are "keep". That's lovely on a slow Sunday and painful when your phone says storage is full and you need to take a video *now*.

{app}'s **Swipe to clean** starts with a scan, then shows you only the photos that are likely deletes:

- **Extra copies** of the same shot, with the best one always kept
- **Blurry and black shots**, like pocket photos
- **Receipts and documents** you photographed once and never needed again
- **Screenshots** over a month old

They're sorted **biggest first**, so the first few swipes free the most space. Swipe left to delete, right to keep, undo anytime, and everything you chose is deleted together at the end. It's free, with no ads and no countdown.

## The photos you keep can shrink too

Swiping only deletes. Often the biggest win is making the photos you love smaller:

- **Live Photos** each carry a hidden video clip. Turning them into stills keeps the photo and drops the clip. Here's [how much space Live Photos take](/guides/live-photos-storage-iphone).
- **4K videos** can be re-saved at the same quality or 1080p, and trimmed first.
- **Screenshots** are saved as large PNGs, and {app} re-saves the keepers at a fraction of the size.

Swipewipe's subscription can compress and trim videos, but it doesn't advertise Live Photo conversion or screenshot re-saving. In {app}, all of these keep the date, location, and favorite status.

## Ads, timers, and price

Swipewipe's help page explains that the free version "limits the number of swipes": when you reach it, a countdown starts, or you can watch a short video for more. Its App Store listing is marked **Contains Advertising**, and some reviewers mention waits of several hours between months. The subscription removes both. The US App Store lists weekly prices from $3.99 to $9.99 and a $29.99 yearly plan.

{app}'s free swiping has **no ads and no limit**. Pro is **{annual} a year**, or {weekly} for a week, and it adds the shrinking tools and one-tap Photo Cleanup.

## Privacy

Swipewipe's App Store label lists **identifiers, usage data, and other data as "Data Used to Track You"**, and purchases as linked to you. {app} has no ads, no analytics, and no network connections at all, so its label is **Data Not Collected**.
