Slidebox was swiping photos before swiping photos was a trend. Built in Seattle by former Amazon developers, it launched on iPhone in 2015 and on Android a year later, and it carries an Apple **Editors' Choice** badge. On the US App Store it averages 4.8 stars from about 17,000 ratings.

It's a different kind of app from {app}, and that's the key to choosing between them.

## What Slidebox does well

- **Organizing is a joy.** Swipe up to trash, swipe down to favorite, tap an album name to file a photo. Reviewers describe sorting thousands of photos in an afternoon.
- **A good privacy record.** Its App Store label lists **no data used to track you** and none linked to you.
- **Pay once, if you like.** There's a one-time unlock, and Slidebox says it doesn't do free trials.
- **It's everywhere**: iPhone, iPad, Apple-silicon Macs, and Android.

If your goal is a tidy library with every photo in the right album, Slidebox is one of the best tools there is, and {app} doesn't try to compete.

## Organizing vs. freeing space

The trouble starts when the goal is storage. Slidebox shows you photos in order, and you decide on each one. That's perfect for sorting, but it's slow for finding what's actually wasting space, because a pocket shot from last week and a 4 GB video from 2022 get the same one swipe each, in date order.

{app} starts with a scan of your whole library. **Swipe to clean** then shows only the likely deletes, **biggest first**:

- extra copies of the same shot (the best one always stays)
- blurry and black shots
- receipts and documents
- screenshots over a month old

Swipe left to delete, right to keep, with undo. It's free and unlimited, and because the biggest items come first, the first few swipes do most of the work.

## Shrinking the photos you keep

Slidebox can only help you delete. But most of what fills an iPhone is stuff worth keeping: long videos, Live Photos, and years of screenshots. With Pro, {app} makes those smaller instead:

- **4K videos** re-saved at the same quality or 1080p, and trimmed to the part you want.
- **Live Photos** turned into stills, dropping the hidden video clip. Here's [why Live Photos take so much space](/guides/live-photos-storage-iphone).
- **Screenshots** re-saved from PNG to HEIC at a fraction of the size.

Each one keeps its date, location, favorite status, and album, and the original waits in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

## Price and privacy

Slidebox's App Store description lists **$4.99 a month or $49.99 a year**, plus a permanent one-time unlock, and its in-app purchase list shows a range of other weekly, yearly, and lifetime prices. Its listing is still marked **Contains Advertising**, though its latest release notes say "No ads".

{app}'s free version has no ads, and Pro is **{annual} a year** or {weekly} for one week. On privacy, both apps keep it clean: Slidebox tracks nothing, and {app} goes all the way to **Data Not Collected**, with no network connections at all.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose Slidebox if** you want to organize your library into albums, you like doing it by hand, or you want one app across iPhone, Mac, and Android.

**Choose {app} if** your iPhone is full and you want the app to find what's wasting space, biggest first, and shrink what you'd rather keep. They also work well together: free up space with {app}, then tidy what's left with Slidebox.
