{app} and CleanMy®Phone both start from your photo library, but they're built for different goals. Here's the comparison.

## What CleanMy®Phone does well

MacPaw makes careful, good-looking software, and CleanMy®Phone shows it:

- **Organize** sorts your library into AI categories like Travel, People, Pets, Food, and Documents, which is genuinely useful if your camera roll is chaos.
- **Health** gives your phone a score, a security check, storage analytics, and a speed test, free for everyone.
- **Contacts and email**: merge duplicate contacts and unsubscribe from newsletters.
- It works on **iPhone and iPad**, and it's included in **Setapp** if you already subscribe.

## What's free, and what isn't

This is where the two apps differ most.

CleanMy®Phone's free version covers the **Largest Videos** and **Screenshots** categories, the **Other** category in Organize, and the Health module, according to MacPaw's own support page. The features most people install a cleaner for, finding **duplicates and similar photos**, sit behind the subscription, along with blurred photos, Live Photo conversion, and video compression.

In {app}, finding duplicates is free. **Swipe to clean** puts every likely delete in front of you, biggest first: extra copies (the best one always stays), blurry and black shots, receipts, documents, and old screenshots. Swipe left to delete, right to keep, with undo, and everything you chose is deleted together at the end. The best shot of each burst, the Files cleanup, and every step-by-step guide are free too.

Pro is for the heavy lifting: shrinking and trimming videos, Live Photos to stills, screenshot re-saves, and clearing everything in one tap.

## Shrinking, not just sorting

CleanMy®Phone added video compression and Live Photo conversion in 2025, and both are worth having. {app} takes the same idea further:

- **Trim, then shrink.** Long recordings can be cut to the part you want before they're compressed.
- **Screenshots re-saved.** iPhone screenshots are PNG files. {app} re-saves them as smaller HEIC copies with the same pixels, which adds up fast if you keep thousands.
- **Details kept.** Every shrunk video and converted Live Photo keeps its date, location, favorite status, and album, and {app} checks each one afterwards.

If you're wondering how much Live Photos really cost you, our [Live Photos storage guide](/guides/live-photos-storage-iphone) breaks it down.

## Price

CleanMy®Phone costs **$7.99 a month or $36.99 a year** on the US App Store, after a 3-day trial that renews automatically. MacPaw also sells a lifetime option, though its terms say it doesn't include major upgrades or new features released after you buy.

{app} Pro is **{annual} a year**, less than CleanMy®Phone's yearly plan, or **{weekly} for a single week** if you just need one big cleanup. There's no trial to cancel, because the free scan already shows how much Pro would save on your phone.

## Privacy

Both apps process your photos on the device. CleanMy®Phone's App Store label lists **identifiers and usage data as "Data Used to Track You"**. {app} has no analytics, no ads, and no network connections, so its label is **Data Not Collected**.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose CleanMy®Phone if** you want your library organized into categories, you use an iPad too, or you already have Setapp.

**Choose {app} if** you want duplicates found without a subscription, if your space is mostly videos and Live Photos you'd rather keep, or if you want a measured before-and-after instead of an estimate.
