Videos are almost always the biggest files on an iPhone. If you don't want to delete them, the next best thing is making them smaller.

## How big are iPhone videos?

Apple lists rough sizes in **Settings → Camera → Record Video**. With the High Efficiency format, one minute of video is approximately:

| Setting | Approx. size per minute |
| --- | --- |
| 720p HD at 30 fps | 40 MB |
| 1080p HD at 30 fps | 60 MB |
| 1080p HD at 60 fps | 90 MB |
| 4K at 30 fps | 190 MB |
| 4K at 60 fps | 400 MB |

So a five-minute 4K/60 clip is about **2 GB**, and the same clip at 1080p/30 is about **300 MB**. Check your own iPhone's screen for exact numbers. They vary by model and format.

## Method 1: Trim what you don't need

The easiest compression is cutting the boring parts.

1. Open the video in **Photos** and tap **Edit**.
2. Drag the handles at either end of the timeline to keep only the part you want.
3. Tap **Done**, then **Save Video as New Clip**.
4. Delete the original if you no longer need it.

Saving as a new clip creates a genuinely smaller file. Choosing **Save Video** keeps the original underneath so you can revert, which doesn't save as much space.

## Method 2: Re-encode with the Shortcuts app

The free **Shortcuts** app (built into iOS) can re-encode a video at a lower resolution.

1. Open **Shortcuts** and tap **+** to create a shortcut.
2. Add the **Select Photos** action and leave it set to videos.
3. Add **Encode Media**. Tap **Show More** and set **Size** to **1920×1080** (or **1280×720** for smaller files).
4. Add **Save to Photo Album**.
5. Run the shortcut, pick a video, and check the new, smaller copy.
6. Once you're happy with it, delete the original.

## Method 3: Record smaller videos from now on

Go to **Settings → Camera**:

- **Record Video:** choose **1080p HD at 30 fps** for everyday clips. Switch to 4K only when you need it.
- **Formats:** choose **High Efficiency**, which records HEVC video that's much smaller than the older "Most Compatible" format.
- **Record Slo-mo:** a lower setting also helps if you use slo-mo a lot.

## Don't forget Recently Deleted

When you delete original videos, they move to **Recently Deleted** and keep using storage for about 30 days. Empty it from **Photos → Utilities → Recently Deleted** once you've checked your compressed copies.
