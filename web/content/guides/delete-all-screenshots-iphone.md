Screenshots are the clutter nobody notices until there are three thousand of them: receipts, boarding passes, recipes, memes, and things you meant to look at later. They're easy to clear in bulk.

## Delete screenshots in bulk

1. Open **Photos**.
2. Scroll down to **Media Types** and tap **Screenshots**. This album contains only screenshots, so you can't accidentally select a normal photo.
3. Tap **Select** in the top-right corner.
4. Tap the first screenshot you want to delete, **keep your finger down, and drag** across and down. Everything you pass over gets selected, and the grid scrolls as you go.
5. Tap the **trash** icon and confirm.

If your iOS version shows **Select All** after you tap Select, you can use that instead. Just un-tap the few you want to keep before deleting.

## Save the ones you need first

Before a bulk delete, take a minute to rescue the useful ones:

- **Favorite** anything you want to keep (tap the heart), so it's easy to spot and skip.
- Move important ones, like IDs or confirmations, to a dedicated **album** or the **Notes** app.
- Anything with sensitive information is better stored somewhere protected than in your camera roll.

## Free the space

Deleted screenshots go to **Recently Deleted** and keep using storage for about 30 days. To free the space immediately:

1. In Photos, go to **Utilities → Recently Deleted**.
2. Unlock with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.
3. Tap **Select → Delete All**.

## Stop them piling up

When you take a screenshot, a preview appears in the corner. Tap it, mark it up or share it, then tap **Done** and choose **Delete Screenshot**. It never makes it into your library.
