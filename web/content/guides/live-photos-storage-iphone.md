Live Photos are on by default on iPhone, so most people have thousands of them without ever choosing to. Each one is a little bigger than it looks.

## What's inside a Live Photo

When you take a Live Photo, your iPhone saves two things: the still image you see, and a short video covering roughly a second and a half before and after you pressed the shutter. That's what plays when you press and hold the photo, and what powers the Loop, Bounce, and Long Exposure effects.

For a few special moments, that's lovely. For a screenshot of a menu, a photo of a parking spot, or the 30 near-identical pictures from a dinner, it's extra storage you'll never use.

## Why turning Live off in Edit doesn't help

The obvious fix is to open a photo, tap **Edit**, and tap the **Live** button to turn it off. That changes how the photo displays, but it **doesn't save space**. Photos edits are non-destructive: the original video is kept so you can turn Live back on later.

To actually reclaim the space, you need a still copy and you need to delete the original.

## How to keep Live Photos as stills

1. Open **Photos** and go to **Media Types → Live Photos** to see only your Live Photos.
2. Tap **Select** and choose the photos you want to convert.
3. Tap the **More** (**•••**) button, then **Duplicate**.
4. When asked, choose **Duplicate as Still Photo**.
5. Check the new still copies, then go back and delete the original Live Photos.
6. Empty **Utilities → Recently Deleted** to free the space.

The still copies keep the picture you saw when you took it. Live effects like Loop and Bounce won't be available on them.

## Which Live Photos to keep

Before converting in bulk, pull out the ones where the motion matters: kids, pets, fireworks, anything you might want to turn into a Loop or Long Exposure. **Favorite** them first, then skip favorites when selecting.

## Stop new ones piling up

If you rarely use the motion, turn Live Photos off going forward:

1. In the **Camera** app, tap the **Live** button so it's crossed out.
2. Go to **Settings → Camera → Preserve Settings** and turn on **Live Photo**. Without this, iOS turns Live back on the next time you open the camera.

You can still turn Live on for a single shot whenever you want.
