# The Trap That Caught Its Maker: voice script for Gemini TTS

Paste the **Style** into the Style field and the script block into one speech block. Tags in angle brackets are
Gemini's inline expression tags; everything else is read word for word. Numbers are spelled out so they're read right.

## Speaker and style
- **Voice:** Charon, the account's narrator from the Geniza, Smithsonian, Vivian and squirrel shorts.
- **Style:**
  > Calm, low male storyteller narrating a dark true story for a short animated video. Fast and steady, about 220 words
  > a minute, with almost no pauses between sentences. Quiet and matter-of-fact, a little grim, never spooky, never
  > dramatic or announcer-like. Let the short lines land plainly ("He had starved.", "Langley was nowhere."). Keep
  > the same tone and pace all the way through, including the last two lines.

## Script
```
A man booby-trapped his whole house to keep out burglars. <short pause> The trap caught him instead.
And upstairs, his blind brother was still waiting for dinner.
Harlem, New York. Homer Collyer was a lawyer. His little brother Langley played piano at Carnegie Hall.
Then Homer went blind, and Langley stayed home to look after him.
He fed him a hundred oranges a week, sure it would bring his sight back.
And he saved every newspaper, so Homer could catch up on the news the day he could see again.
The bills went unpaid. The phone, the lights, the water and the gas were all cut off. The piles kept growing.
Fourteen pianos. Twenty-five thousand books. Pickled organs in jars. Most of an old Model T.
People said there was cash hidden inside, so Langley dug tunnels through the junk and strung them with tripwires.
Then one day, somebody called the police. There was a dead man in the house.
It took five hours to dig to him. It was Homer. He had starved.
Langley was nowhere. People said he'd run away.
Eighteen days later, workers found him ten feet from his brother, crushed under his own trap, on his way to bring Homer food.
They hauled more than a hundred tons out of that house.
Firefighters still call a home packed like that a Collyer mansion.
<short pause> Langley saved every page for a day that never came.
Your phone does it too. Thousands of photos, saved for someday.
SimpleShrink cleans up gigabytes in minutes. Link in bio.
```
